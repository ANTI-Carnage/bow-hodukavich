# B.O.W.: Hodukavich

An unofficial, fan-made browser game inspired by Resident Evil (2026). You play as Bryan Hodukavich **after** his mutation: a multi-limbed mass of mutated flesh with a huge bloodshot eye bulging from the side of his head and a crown of writhing tentacles. Absorb scientists and soldiers into your body, grow bigger with every one, evolve between levels, and break through the military blockade.

**Play it:** https://bow-hodukavich.sites.tab.bot/ (also on GitHub Pages: https://anti-carnage.github.io/bow-hodukavich/)

Works on phones (touch) and on computers and Chromebooks (keyboard). No install, no downloads.

## Controls

| | Keyboard | Phone / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys | Drag anywhere on the screen (joystick) |
| Tentacle lash (melee sweep) | J or SPACE (hold to keep lashing) | LASH button |
| Tendril grab (ranged) | K | GRAB button |
| Eruption roar (special) | L when the MUTAGEN bar is full | ROAR button (glows when ready) |
| Pause | P or Esc | II button (top right) |
| Sound on/off | M | Speaker button |
| Pick an evolution | 1 / 2 / 3 | Tap a card |

## How it plays

- **Absorb** humans to heal, fill your MUTAGEN meter and grow: your blob gets bigger, the eye swells, and more tentacles and absorbed arms sprout from the mass. Scientists can be absorbed any time (they run). Soldiers have to be knocked **DOWN** first (or weakened to half health and grabbed). Anything with a pulsing green ring can be absorbed.
- **Lash** sweeps your tentacles in an arc. It also swats incoming bullets out of the air.
- **Grab** fires a tendril at the nearest target. Weak or downed humans get pulled straight in.
- **Eruption roar** (meter full) blasts everything around you, stuns every human in range (so you can absorb them) and wipes out bullets and incoming strikes.
- Absorb several in a row for a **combo** score multiplier.
- **Enemies:** fleeing scientists, rifle soldiers (watch for the red laser sight before they fire), riot-shield troopers (block attacks from the front, hit them from the side or roar), flamethrower troopers, and night drones whose spotlight calls in air strikes (get out of the red circle).
- **Levels:** Quarantine Lab, Containment Wing, Military Blockade, Evac Zone (night), then the boss: the **Warden Mk-II** walker mech (gatling sweeps, missile barrages, stomps). When it overheats, its core is exposed and takes double damage.
- Between levels you **evolve**: pick one of three mutations (extra tentacles, speed, armored flesh, longer reach, thick hide, regeneration, and more).
- Beat the Warden to become the **Apex Organism**, then keep mutating through harder loops. Your best score is saved on your device.

## About

Fan game for fun, not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom. All art is drawn in code and all sounds are generated live with WebAudio; no assets from the official games are used. No gore: absorbed humans just get pulled into the mass.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step: open `index.html` or serve the folder.
